## How to use this file

This is the **price list** and the **rates of record**. Everything here is safe to quote to a client, and this is the file to use whenever pricing needs to be communicated — an enquiry, a change of arrangement, a rate update, an invoice question.

**When writing anything a client will read:**

- Quote rates only from the tables below. Do not calculate, estimate, or infer a rate that is not written here.
- If a client's situation is not covered — an unusual frequency, a discount request, a legacy rate, a format not listed — say so and ask Karl. Do not invent a number.
- Do not discuss what a session earns, revenue per slot, margins, how rates are constructed, or how they compare to competitors. None of that belongs in client-facing writing, and none of it is in this file.
- Do not describe any option as a worse deal, inefficient, or a way of encouraging a client toward something else. Present the options plainly and let the client choose.
- Prices include GST. Say so.

## Terms

- **Minimum term:** 12 weeks, unless agreed otherwise in writing.
- **Session cancellation:** 24 hours. Sessions cancelled with 24 hours' notice or more are rescheduled where possible. Sessions cancelled inside 24 hours are forfeited.
- **Direct debit cancellation:** 30 days' notice, by email.
- **Payment:** weekly direct debit through Ezidebit unless otherwise agreed; direct deposit can be arranged. Ezidebit transaction fees are charged in addition to the agreed amount and appear on the debit.
- **Holidays and pauses:** clients going away can cancel sessions and drop to $22 per week for app access, keeping their programming running.
- **Trial:** a complimentary first session is often available.

### Shared sessions (2-on-1 and 3-on-1)

- Each participant has their own agreement and their own payment arrangement, and is not responsible for anyone else's fees.
- If another participant cannot attend, your rate does not change. The session runs as scheduled for whoever attends.
- If a participant leaves permanently, you continue at your agreed rate for two weeks while a replacement is found. Karl will then confirm in writing the rate for the new group size. Your rate changes only if you agree in writing, and you may end the agreement at that point without further notice or fee.

## Presenting these rates

When a client asks for pricing, lead with the format they asked about, showing both rates. Then app access at $22 per week, noting it costs nothing extra at one session a week. Then online coaching for anyone who wants programming without a session commitment.

Keep app access as a single line item — it is one thing, and splitting programming from app access reads as two charges.

If asked why the with-app session rate is lower: the app fee replaces the difference. At one session a week it costs the same either way, and beyond that it works out cheaper.
