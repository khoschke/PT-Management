## What is deliberately left out

- **Change-email.** It works and has now been run end to end, but it is a
  two-link flow where both the old and the new address have to confirm, and it
  is fiddly enough to deserve its own note rather than one line here. Telling
  four people about it in passing buys four support questions.
- **The staff development pathway.** Real, and live, but it is about staff
  moving toward trainer status, not about these four.
- **Anything about rates, rent or commercial terms.** See the figure rule in
  `CLAUDE.md`. Nothing in this email goes near it, and nothing added to it
  should.

## Why the pause section is worded the way it is

It would be easy to trim, and it should not be. The PTs are being handed a way
to say no, and if it is not explicitly blessed they will read using it as
admitting they cannot cope, and they will not use it. Naming pausing as the
honest option, and a cold lead as the actual failure, is what makes it safe to
use. That framing is the point of the paragraph, not the mechanics above it.

## The email

Hi Dylan, Julie, Shahd and Michael,

A few things have gone live in the PT Portal since my last note. None of it
changes how leads reach you. It is mostly about giving you control of things you
used to have to ask me for.

**Your profile is yours now**

Sign in at pt.fitazgym.com/admin and you will see "My profile" in the menu. That
page is yours to edit any time:

- Your specialties
- A short bio on how you train and what you are good at
- Whether you take morning sessions, evening sessions, or both

You no longer need to come to me to change any of it.

**Your specialties decide which leads get suggested to you**

Worth being honest here rather than ticking everything. When a lead comes in,
the system suggests a trainer partly by matching that member's goals against
your specialties. If everyone tags everything, the matching stops meaning
anything and it comes down to whoever has the fewest leads on.

Tag what you are genuinely best at. Leave the rest.

**If your book is full, you can pause**

Untick both Morning and Evening and you are paused. That means:

- You will not be suggested for new leads
- Members will not see you on the booking form
- Your existing leads stay exactly where they are

You will see a reminder at the top of every page while you are paused, and one
click turns it back on.

Please use it. If you are full, pausing is the honest way to say so and it costs
you nothing. What does cost us is a lead sitting on someone's board going cold.
So if you are at capacity, pause it, and let me know.

**You can reset your own password**

There is a "Forgot password?" link on the sign in page now. No need to wait on
me.

**What I would like from you**

Sign in this week, open My profile, and check it reads the way you would want a
member to hear it. Fix anything out of date. If something does not work, tell
me.

Thanks,
Karl
