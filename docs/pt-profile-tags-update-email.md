## The email

Hi Dylan, Julie, Shahd and Michael,

Quick one. I've made two changes to your profile in the PT Portal, and I need
five minutes from each of you this week to make them work.

**New tags on your profile**

Until now every tag was a goal: fat loss, strength and so on. Nothing said who
you actually work best with, so the matching couldn't tell Julie's older
adults from Dylan's runners. There are now two new groups:

- **Who you work with:** beginners and people getting back into it, older
  adults, women's health, pre and post natal, peri-menopause and menopause,
  teens and youth, runners and endurance athletes, physique and competition
  prep.
- **How you work:** online coaching, nutrition coaching, and small group and
  partner training (up to 3).

Members now get the same options on the request form, in plain language
("I'm new to exercise, or getting back into it", "Help with my nutrition"),
and a lead gets matched to the trainers who share their tags.

Same rule as last time: tick what you're genuinely best at and leave the rest.
If everyone ticks everything, the matching means nothing. Only tick small
group and partner training if your business actually offers it.

**Your bio is now the thing members read**

The box under your tags has a new name: "How a member should hear about you".
It's what front of house reads when someone asks about PT, so it needs to sound
like you talking to a member, not a list. Two or three sentences: who you work
best with, what you help them do, and how you work. Anything the tags don't
cover (rehab, boxing, kettlebells, your certifications) goes in here too.

**What to do, by Friday**

Sign in at pt.fitazgym.com/admin, open My profile, tick your new tags, and
give your bio a read with fresh eyes. Five minutes.

Until you tick them, a member who picks one of the new options can't be matched
to you on it, so it's worth doing sooner rather than later.

Cheers,
