## The email

Hi Dylan, Julie, Shahd and Michael,

Over the last couple of days I've rewritten Part 7 of the workbook (Making
Sales), and built the portal to match it. The short version: the portal now
remembers your follow-ups for you, and you can see your own numbers. Ten
minutes to read this, and two small things to do this week.

**1. Three kinds of gym lead, plus your own**

- **Warm (form):** asked for PT themselves through the form. Same as before.
- **Flagged:** someone I've added after a chat on the floor, an email or a
  heads-up from the front desk. They've shown some interest, so they're not
  cold. Check the notes for why I flagged them.
- **Unverified (cold):** a new member who hasn't asked about PT. They reach you
  around day 10 to 17, after two of the gym's three welcome emails. These are
  the only cold leads now, and Part 7 has new cold call and cold text scripts
  for them that mention those emails.

**Add my own lead** is a new button on your lead board for people you find
yourself: on the floor, through a referral, on social. Pick where you found
them and they're yours, with the usual 48-hour clock. If the portal says the
member's already in there, check with me before contacting them. Someone else
may be looking after them, or they may have asked not to be contacted.

**2. The 2-2-2 now runs itself**

Once you've made first contact, the portal works out when each follow-up is
due and tells you: on the lead, in a short email on any morning something's
due, and in your Monday email. Send the touch, tap **Done**, and the next one
is scheduled. There's a **Follow-ups due** filter on your board too.

When someone says no, it works differently, and this is the part I need you to
get right:

- Set the lead to **Said no**. That starts a 48-hour clock.
- Ask once, in the same conversation: "Totally fine. Mind if I check in down the
  track, or would you rather I leave it there?"
- Record their answer on the lead: **They said yes to a check-in**, **They said
  leave it there**, or **Asked by text, waiting**.
- A yes puts them on the 2-2-2. Anything else closes the lead, and they're not
  contacted about PT again.

If nothing's recorded within 48 hours, the portal closes the lead and lets me
know. If you did ask in time and just didn't update the portal, tell me and I'll
reopen it. That's not a black mark. It's there so nobody gets chased after
they've said no.

**3. Closing a lead is now one step, with a confirm**

Picking **Not interested**, or using **End communication** when someone replies
STOP or asks to be left alone, brings up a short confirm explaining what
happens. Once confirmed, it adds a dated note to the lead. It can't be undone
from your end, so if you close one by mistake, let me know.

**4. Every first text now ends with "Reply STOP to opt out"**

These texts promote a service, so under the Spam Act they count as commercial
messages, even though the session's free. Put the line on every first-contact
text. You don't need it on replies once you're talking. If someone replies
STOP, that's the end of it. Record it on the lead the same day.

**5. A quick reflection after each sales chat**

When a lead moves on from Attended, a short reflection pops up at the top of
the lead: how it went, where it stalled, which certainty was missing, and one
line on what you'd do differently. If you'd rather talk than type, use the
microphone on your keyboard. It takes thirty seconds and it's optional. Only
you and I see it. It's there because fifty conversations with honest reflection
make you a lot better at this than fifty without.

**6. My numbers**

There's a new **My numbers** tab with your own figures: how many of your leads
you contacted within 48 hours, booked, saw at their session and signed up, and
which of the places you find leads actually turn into clients. It shows your
numbers only. No leaderboard, no comparing with each other. The comparison that
matters is with your own last month. Small numbers swing around a lot, so look
at the counts as well as the percentages.

**7. The workbook**

It's now called the **PT Workbook**, because it's for all of us, not just new
starters. Part 7 has been rewritten, and opens with a one-page **How a lead
moves through the portal**. Part 6 now points you to My numbers for tracking
your marketing. Links in the workbook open in a new tab, so you don't lose your
place.

**The two things I need from you this week**

1. **Read the first page of Part 7,** How a lead moves through the portal. It's
   one page, and it's the whole process in one place.
2. **Start the 2-2-2 on any lead you've contacted who's gone quiet.** Leads
   contacted before this week don't have it running yet. Open the lead, and in
   the Follow-up section tap **Start the 2-2-2**.

As always, anything that doesn't fit how you actually work with your leads,
tell me. A lot of this came straight from what you've told me, and the rest
will get better the same way.

Cheers,

## Notes on the draft

- **The no / permission question section is the operationally important
  part**, so it gets the most words and the plain "this is the part I need you
  to get right". The 48-hour timer close is the one thing that can catch a PT
  out, so it's framed as protecting the member, not policing the trainer, and
  says plainly that a missed update can be reopened.
- **The two asks are deliberately small and concrete.** "Start the 2-2-2" is
  needed because the schedule only begins for leads contacted after `0022`
  went live (`docs/PROJECT_STATUS.md`, Watch).
- **My numbers says "only you and I" and "no leaderboard" up front**, so nobody
  reads a new metrics tab as performance management.
- **Reflections are sold on why, not on the feature**, in one sentence, in the
  language the workbook already uses (Part 7's closing section).
- **Spam Act is stated as the reason, not a lecture.** The detail is in Part 7.
- **Not mentioned on purpose:** the database changes, the PT Manager's Trainer
  filter on Metrics (Karl's coaching tool; worth saying in person if asked,
  never as "I can see your numbers"), and the browser Speak button that was
  tried and dropped.
- **Check before sending:** the Monday email reference assumes the weekly
  summary is running (first send 23 Nov 2026). If this goes out before then,
  "in your Monday email" still holds once it starts.
