## 2. How the structure works

Three rules govern every number above. Any new rate should be derived from them, not invented.

1. **Every price is a round ex-GST figure plus 10%.** $110 = $100, $88 = $80, $77 = $70, $66 = $60, $55 = $50, $44 = $40, $22 = $20.
2. **The "+ programming" column is always the PT-only price minus $22** — the app fee, carved out of the session price rather than added to it. At weekly frequency this makes programming effectively free: $88 + $22 = $110, and $55 + $22 = $77. The client pays the same either way, but stays engaged all week.
3. **The commitment ladder is a flat step per tier:** $16.50 on 1-on-1, $11 on 2-on-1 (15% and 14.3% of the weekly rate respectively). Less commitment costs more — the rate is never discounted for higher frequency.

### Revenue per 45-minute slot

## 4. Standard terms

### General

- **Program commitment:** programs are usually structured around a **12-week** minimum. Can be waived at Karl's discretion for clients easing in.
- **Direct debit cancellation:** 30 days' notice, by email.
- **Payments:** weekly direct debit via Ezidebit. **Ezidebit transaction fees are charged on top** of the prices above (e.g. a client paying $198/week is debited $202.44 — $4.44 in fees).
- **Pauses / holidays:** clients going away can cancel sessions and drop to **$22/week app access** to keep their programming running.
- **Trials:** first session is often offered as a complimentary trial.

### Session cancellation

- **24 hours' notice or more:** the session is rescheduled where possible.
- **Inside 24 hours:** the session is forfeited.

### Shared sessions (2-on-1 and 3-on-1)

- **Each person is billed separately, on their own direct debit.** Never one payer covering both. It gets messy the moment one person leaves, and it makes the remaining client feel they have inherited someone else's bill.
- **If one person can't attend, nobody else's cost changes.** A 2-on-1 that becomes a 1-on-1 for the day, or a 3-on-1 that becomes a 2-on-1, runs at the normal rate for everyone attending. No top-ups, no adjustments.

  *Rationale (for explaining to clients):* charging the difference on the day is awkward and sours the session. The premium a shared slot earns over a 1-on-1 — $154 or $198 against $110 — is the insurance for those occasions. Lost income on the day, absorbed by the format over time.

- **If one person leaves permanently,** the remaining client(s) move up one tier after a **two-week grace period** to find a replacement:
  - 3-on-1 → 2-on-1 rate
  - 2-on-1 → 1-on-1 rate

## 5. Recommended presentation order

When quoting a full price list to a prospect, present it in this order (workshopped Nov 2025 to remove confusion):

1. **PT only (no online programming)** — anchors the standalone rate first
2. **Online programming ($22/wk) + PT session** — shows what changes when bundled
3. **Online programming only** — for anyone who just wants structure and accountability

Leading with PT-only pre-empts the "why is the bundle cheaper?" question. Don't split app access and online programming into separate lines — they are the same thing, and listing them separately reads as two charges.

**If asked why the bundle is cheaper than PT-only:** it's a bundled rate. More touchpoints across the week means better client outcomes, so the per-service cost comes down. PT-only is the standalone rate.

## 6. Positioning and rationale

- **Frequency discounts run the opposite way to most PTs.** Don't reduce the per-session rate for higher weekly frequency. Clients who train 3x/week were going to do so largely regardless of price, and discounting devalues the service. Set the base rate at the minimum you'd accept, then add a premium for lower-frequency options. That produces a tiered structure without ever undervaluing your time.
- **Legacy rates are hard to undo.** Once a client is on a discounted rate for an open-ended period, it is very difficult to bring them back up. Move clients to current rates at a natural transition point — change of arrangement, new year, change of frequency.
- **Free app access** is extended only to family and a couple of close friends. A deliberate privilege, not a default.
- **Market context (Brisbane, 2025 assessment):** non-gym-affiliated PTs average $80–$100 for 45–60 min; gym-affiliated PTs $65–$90 without app-based programming; online coaching ranges $15–$80/wk. Differentiators are tertiary qualifications in exercise science *and* behavioural science, plus habit and behaviour-change coaching rather than sessions alone.
