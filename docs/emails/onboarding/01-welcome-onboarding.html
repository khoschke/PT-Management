Step 2 - Download the Fitaz Gym App
The Fitaz Gym app is available on iOS and Android.
Step 3 - Log in and book your first class
After downloading the Fitaz app, log in using your email as your username and your password. If you haven't set one yet, tap Forgot password.
Once you're in, book your first class through the app or use the button below to book via the web portal. Even Base members receive 2 weeks of unlimited classes to get settled in.
Book your first class
Step 4 - Accessing the gym
Smartphone access. No fobs required. Once you've downloaded the Fitaz Gym app, simply tap Check In when you're within 5 metres of the main entrance to unlock the door. This will be your access method moving forward, giving you seamless entry every time you visit.
Step 5 - Join our Facebook community group
In this group you can connect with other members, share your progress, and stay up to date with gym announcements.
