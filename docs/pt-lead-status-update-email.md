## The email

Hi Dylan, Julie, Shahd and Michael,

A few changes to the PT portal this week that affect how you update your leads.
Five minutes, and one small thing I need back from you.

**The statuses have changed**

Two have been renamed and one is new. In funnel order:

- **Contacted:** you've reached out (call, text or email), whether or not they
  replied.
- **Comp. Booked:** a date is locked in for the complimentary session. This used
  to be "Booked".
- **Attended:** the session happened, and they haven't decided yet. This used to
  be "Completed".
- **Converted:** they've signed up to PT with you. New.
- **Not interested:** they said no.
- **Unreachable:** you tried and never got a reply. Same as before, and they
  stay in the member email flow on purpose.

You don't need to memorise these. Hover over a status, or look under the
dropdown when you pick one, and the meaning is right there.

The reason for the change is simple. Until now a booked session and an attended
session looked the same, so we couldn't see the biggest drop-off in the whole
process: people who book and don't turn up. That's a different problem from
people who come and decide it's not for them, and we can't fix either one if we
can't see which is which.

**Two habits that make it work**

1. **Tick through Attended, even if they decide on the spot.** If someone signs
   up at the end of the session, move them to Attended and then Converted. It's
   one extra tap, and it's what tells a no-show apart from a no.
2. **Treat Attended as a waiting room.** Once they've made a decision, move them
   on to Converted or Not interested.

**The one thing I need from you this week**

Every lead that was on "Completed" is now on "Attended". Some of those people
have probably already signed up or said no. Please go through yours and move
anyone who's decided to Converted or Not interested. It's two minutes, and it
means the numbers start out right.

**A new Log contact button**

If you follow up with someone without changing their status (a second call, a
reminder text), open the lead and tap **Log contact**. It keeps your "last
contacted" date honest, which matters for the next part.

**A Monday morning email, from 23 November**

From next Monday, around 7am, you'll get a short email listing your active
leads: each one's status, when you last contacted them and when they were
assigned to you. Anyone you haven't contacted for a while is at the top. It also
flags any lead that's been sitting on Comp. Booked or Attended for more than a
week, with the next step to take.

This one came from Dylan, so thank him. The daily digest I get only tells me
when a lead has already gone past 48 hours. This tells you what needs a nudge
before it gets there. A copy comes to me as well, so I can see where I can help.

If you have no active leads, you'll still get it, with a line saying so. That's
how you know it's running and nothing's broken.

Your first one may flag a few leads, mostly Attended ones that were decided a
while ago and never updated. Updating the status clears the flag.

Anything in here that doesn't fit how you actually work with your leads, tell
me. You're the ones using it every day.

Cheers,

## Notes on the draft

- **The "this week" ask is the operationally important part.** The migration
  renamed every Completed lead to Attended, because the portal can't tell which
  trainers meant "came to the session" and which meant "signed up". Only the PTs
  know. Until they sort it, the conversion numbers undercount and the Monday
  email flags leads that are already decided.
- **Credit to Dylan is deliberate**, per the leadership skill: public credit when
  someone's contribution improves things for the team. It says nothing about why
  the email matters for any one trainer's leads. That stays private.
- **"A copy comes to me as well"** is said plainly, so nobody finds the CC and
  reads it as surveillance. It's framed as support.
- **Nothing about the My development tab.** It was hidden from trainers in
  October, and nobody has raised it.
- **Unreachable is restated** because the briefing in September made a point of
  it, and a status shake-up is when people's habits drift.
